Our Foundation

Rooted in Community,
Built for Today

Alajo Yankee draws from the West African "Ajo", "Esusu" and "Susu" traditions of rotating savings groups, where members contribute regularly and take turns receiving the full pot. We've digitized this powerful model for diaspora communities in the United States.

Whatever your current persuasions about Africa, the motherland was the cradle of extra-ordinary wealth. Africa had systems/traditions to make & grow wealth that persists till today. Our platform combines cultural trust with modern financial technology, giving families and friends a secure, transparent way to save and grow wealth together, just as their ancestors did.
