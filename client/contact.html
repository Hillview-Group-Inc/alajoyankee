Common Questions

How do I start a savings group?
+

Create a free account, then navigate to "Create Group" in your dashboard. Invite members by email, set your contribution amount and schedule, and you're live in minutes.

Is my money secure on the platform?
+

Yes. We use bank-level encryption, JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, and secure SQL Server infrastructure. Your financial data is never sold or shared.

How long does account setup take?
+

Account creation takes under 2 minutes. You can start inviting members and managing your savings group immediately after email verification.

Do you offer group consulting?
+

Yes! Our consulting service helps established community groups formalize their structure, establish governance, and migrate to our digital platform seamlessly.
